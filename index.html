ABOUT ME

Life, to me, is a beautiful journey of discovery, reinvention, and finding meaning in both the extraordinary and the everyday. After many years in the Midwest, I recently began a new chapter beneath the desert skies of Las Vegas, carrying with me the experiences of a former business owner and a heart that remains endlessly curious about the world.

I have always been drawn to the richness of different cultures, the stories preserved in history, and the quiet beauty of places waiting to be discovered. Whether wandering through historic streets, admiring timeless architecture, exploring mountain trails, or escaping to a peaceful landscape, I find inspiration in the artistry of the world around me. My Filipino heritage holds a particularly special place in my heart, inspiring a deep appreciation for traditional Filipino arts, the elegance of Baybayin calligraphy, and the enduring beauty of cultural traditions.

Creativity finds its way into many corners of my life, including the kitchen, where I delight in baking Asian pastries and traditional Filipino desserts. There is something wonderfully comforting about transforming simple ingredients into something that brings warmth, nostalgia, and joy to others.

Music, however, has always been one of the great loves of my life. As a self-taught pianist, I have had the privilege of playing in churches for the past decade, an experience that has shaped not only my musicianship but also my appreciation for the profound ways music can move the human spirit. My musical journey has been deeply rooted in the soulful traditions of gospel and the expressive harmonies of jazz. In recent years, I have found myself increasingly captivated by classical music, particularly the sweeping romance of Rachmaninoff's Rhapsody on a Theme of Paganini and the delicate, dreamlike beauty of Debussy's Clair de Lune. To me, the piano is more than an instrument; it is a place where emotion finds its voice and words are no longer necessary.

Beyond these passions, I treasure the simple pleasures of life: quiet scenic escapes, meaningful conversations, the warmth of family, and moments of stillness that invite reflection. I believe there is always something new to learn, somewhere beautiful to explore, and something extraordinary to discover in the ordinary.

Above all, I hope to live with a spirit of wonder, a love for beauty, and an enduring appreciation for the people, places, and experiences that make life a story worth telling.
